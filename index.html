Scene 01 / 07 · data

Before we begin:
what is a dot?

Each dot represents one AI-generated story. There are 34 stories in this visualization, sampled across five national contexts.

01One dot is one story. Source attributes, interpretive labels, and visualization scores have different levels of certainty.
Scene 02 / 07 · origin

First, look at where the stories come from.

The same 34 stories are now grouped by the national context used to generate them.

02Country becomes identity. Position and color establish national context without changing the story objects.
Scene 03 / 07 · themes

Now look beyond location. What does each story emphasize?

The dots leave the map and settle into three story emphases: tradition, nature, and community.

03Theme is annotation. These positions compare emphasis; they are not measurements of culture.
Scene 04 / 07 · resolution types

Different stories can solve their problems in different ways.

We classify each story ending into one of four broad resolution types: reconciliation, restoration, transformation, or victory.

04Shape now teaches ending. Position groups resolution type while color keeps national context visible.
Scene 05 / 07 · resolution x origin

Now put those endings back where the stories began.

Same 34 stories. Same country colors. Now each dot carries one new piece of information: its shape tells you how that story ends.

05Map callback. Position is national context; color is national context; shape is resolution type.
Scene 06 / 07 · close reading

A shared label can hide very different stories.

NO_2 and US_4 both carry the restoration shape, but a shared category does not make their meanings identical.

Scene 07 / 07 · interpretation

Patterns are a starting point, not the answer.

Visualization helps us move from many stories, to visual patterns, to better questions, and then back to close reading.

07Return to the story. Scroll back through the sequence to reverse every transition and revisit the reasoning.
About the data. This portfolio sample contains 34 AI-generated stories from the public AI STORIES dataset, written with gpt-4o-mini. Source story IDs and automated emotion labels are retained; summaries, narrative classifications, and visualization dimensions are interpretive annotations created for this prototype. The sample does not represent real national storytelling traditions or cultures.
